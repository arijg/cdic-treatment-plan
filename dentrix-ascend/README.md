# Entering a treatment plan into Dentrix Ascend

Step 3 of `treatment-plan.html` has a **Copy for Dentrix** button. It shows an
entry sheet (one line per tooth: Ascend code, tooth, fee after discounts) and
copies that sheet together with the entry instructions. The coordinator pastes
it into Claude in Chrome with Dentrix Ascend open (`live21.dentrixascend.com`,
already signed in). Claude finds or creates the patient, treatment-plans each
line and reads the totals back.

Everything lives in `treatment-plan.html`:

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Treatment → Ascend code, bundle splits | `DENTRIX_CODES` |
| Custom treatments, X-ray line | `DENTRIX_CATCH_ALL`, `DENTRIX_XRAY` |
| Doctor → provider code, location → office | `DENTRIX_PROVIDERS`, `dentrixOffice()` |
| Per-line fees after discounts | `buildDentrixSheet()` |
| Instructions Claude follows | `DENTRIX_PROMPT` |
| Script that keys the lines into Ascend | `cdicEnterInAscend()` |

## Rules the sheet follows

- Fees are reduced per line so the lines add up to the plan's net total.
  Discounts tied to specific lines come off first (free extractions, courtesy
  items, courtesy X-rays), then arch specials, then whole-case dollars, then
  percentages; each is spread in proportion to what is left on its lines.
- Bundles are split before discounts: on the $5,895 implant post & crown the
  crown is $2,000, the implant gets the rest and the abutment is $0; on the
  titanium implant, abutment & crown the crown is $1,500. Discounts then come
  off the parts proportionally.
- Providers: implant lines (`D6010`) always go under Dr. Goldschlag and crown
  lines (`D2740`, `D6058`) under Dr. Samadi, whoever the plan's doctor is.
  Provider codes differ by office (`DENTRIX_PROVIDERS`): Scarsdale has `DG01`,
  `hedieh`, `DDS1`; Central Park South has `DGCDIC` and `hedieh`. Dr. Horowitz
  never works at Central Park South, so he has no code there; a plan that
  pairs him with that office shows a warning and the script stops. Every other line, and a new patient's primary provider,
  follows the plan's doctor. Abutments, temporaries and Section Crown count as
  "other".
- Codes Ascend defines per mouth (`LBR`, `D9630`, `D6190`, `D9120`, `D0367`,
  `D7953`, `D7952`, `D7921`) are one line with no tooth and the summed fee.
  Ascend saves these without a tooth even when one is selected.
- Lines marked `review` make Claude ask the coordinator, all in one message
  before it enters anything.
- The sheet is ordered for fewest clicks: tooth lines grouped by tooth, then
  no-tooth lines, then lines with no code.

## What was checked in Ascend (2026-11-12)

- A procedure added from Chart > Add Procedure is saved as "Tx Plan", not
  posted, and its Amount can be overridden.
- A tooth must be selected first, and the code search only works under
  "By Categories".

- A seven-line test plan (three-code bundle on one tooth, an extraction, three
  no-tooth lines) was entered on the patient "Test, Test" by following the
  instructions; Tx Planner read back every code, tooth and amount correctly and
  the ledger balance stayed $0.
- The code search only runs when "By Categories" is clicked after typing, and
  new rows do not always land at the top of the procedure list.

- A new patient ("Zztest Claudetest", chart ZC6524B) was created. Required:
  first and last name, gender, birthdate, chart number, address, city, state
  and ZIP. Phone, email and provider are optional.
- A 14-line sheet generated by the form for that patient was entered in full;
  Tx Planner read back all 14 lines and the form's net total ($9,054.00). That
  run covered every mapped code except `D6010`, `D6056`, `D6058`, `D7210` and
  `D9630`, which the first run covered.
- Patient lookup: the simple search by last name lists name and birthdate. The
  advanced search returned nothing for the same patient, so it is not used.

- Not posted: with a $2,200 implant treatment-planned under `DG01`, the
  patient's Ledger showed no transactions and a $0.00 balance, the line's
  status was "Tx Plan", and Tx Planner's Completed count was 0.

- Scripted entry: the `cdicEnterInAscend` script pressed Ascend's own buttons to enter the
  same 14-line sheet in under a minute (against 8 to 10 minutes for Claude in
  Chrome clicking line by line), and Tx Planner read back the same lines and
  total. "Copy for Dentrix" now copies the script with the sheet, and the
  instructions have Claude run it; the click-by-click steps stay as a fallback.

- Claude in Chrome runs (2026-11-12): from the pasted sheet, Claude in Chrome
  entered 14 lines in 1 minute 30 seconds, switched offices both ways, and
  created a new patient. On a forced stop (D7210 with no tooth) the script
  closed its own dialog, Claude reported and waited, and after "skip line 2
  and continue" the remaining lines went in with the right note and total.
  The Ledger stayed at $0.00 each time.
